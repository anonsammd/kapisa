## 🎨 Global Brand Style & Visual Guidelines

- **Primary Colors**: Deep Midnight Navy (`#0E1F3C`), Royal Gold thread accents (`#C9A227`), Pure Clean Alabaster (`#FAF9F6`), Clinical White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Aesthetic**: Premium medical activewear (comparable to *FIGS*, *Lululemon*, and *Vuori* for clinicians). Not stiff or baggy hospital scrubs; modern tailored fits, 4-way stretch draping, athletic postures, clean clinical elegance.
- **Lighting**: High-end fashion commercial studio lighting with soft directional key light, subtle specular rim reflections on fabric micro-texture, and soft diffuse ambient shadows.
- **Models**: Confident healthcare professionals (doctors, surgeons, residents, and nurses), natural expressions, realistic medical badges/stethoscopes.

## 5. Summary Checklist Before Uploading to Shopify

1. **Format**: Save as `.webp` (or `.png` / `.jpg` optimized under 500 KB per image).
2. **Naming**: Use descriptive filenames for SEO (e.g. `kapisa-navy-scrub-set-bestseller.webp`, `kapisa-hospital-fleet-outfitting.webp`).
3. **Alt Text**: Always add descriptive alt text in the Shopify admin (e.g. *"Dr. Aditi Sharma wearing Kapisa Midnight Navy Scrub Top"*).
